💡 Key Analytics & Healthcare Insights

1. Prior Inpatient Visits is the #1 Predictor

Patients with 3+ prior inpatient visits experience a 26.40% 30-day readmission rate — more than 3x the baseline rate (8.59%) for patients with zero prior visits.

2. Post-Acute Care Transition Risk

Discharges to Rehab/Transfers (21.88%) and Skilled Nursing Facilities (14.36%) exhibit significantly higher readmissions than home discharges (9.30%), pointing to care coordination gaps.

3. HbA1c Testing Gap

Over 83% of hospital encounters had no HbA1c test recorded. Tested patients whose diabetes medication was adjusted saw lower readmission rates, emphasizing proactive inpatient glycemic management.

4. Senior Cohort Concentration

Patients aged 70-90 years account for over 42% of total encounters and maintain above-average readmission rates (12.06%–12.57%), indicating high frailty and complex comorbidity loads.
